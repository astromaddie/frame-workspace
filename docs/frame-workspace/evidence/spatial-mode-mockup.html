Spatial Mode: Mockups

Mockups only, for choosing before anything is built. Silver-lavender Palladium in the room, as approved for the window chrome; the bar panel keeps its Steam styling. The window you look at is focused (its frame has a faint blue edge and the Mac cursor is on it). Each window carries its own app's menu bar, because it lives on a display of its own.

1 · Desktop Mode and Spatial Mode

The switch lives where you start the Workspace: on the SteamVR bar panel and on the Mac page. Switching closes one setup and opens the other; the windows you had come back where they were in each mode.

2 · The Dock

Your pinned and running apps with their real icons (letters stand in here). Point and click to open or switch to an app; hold to get Show All Windows, Hide and Quit. The laser lights the icon under it, as on the window chrome.

D1 · Floating ShelfRECOMMENDED
A slim Palladium shelf below your line of sight, in front of you; it follows you when you turn far away. Like the Mac's Dock, in the room.
D2 · Arc Around You
The shelf curves around you at waist height, so apps are always within reach whichever way you face.
D3 · On Your Wrist
Turn your left hand over and the dock appears above the controller; out of sight otherwise.

3 · The App Switcher

Opened with a controller button or Cmd-Tab on the Mac keyboard. Picking a window brings it in front of you and focuses it.

S1 · Strip in Front of YouRECOMMENDED
Live thumbnails of each app's windows in a curved strip at eye level, the current one lit; Cmd-Tab moves along it, as on the Mac.
S2 · Spread Out in Place
Like Mission Control: your windows stay where they are but spread apart, each labelled with its app; look at one and click.
S3 · Apps, Then Windows
A row of app icons; looking at one fans out its windows above it, then pick one.

4 · Live and Still Windows

Only the window you look at, and windows that are changing, stream live; the rest show their last picture (the Frame's decoder can't keep every window live). A still window comes back to life the moment it changes or you look at it, sharpening within a moment.

V1 · No DifferenceRECOMMENDED
Still windows look exactly like live ones; you only ever read the one you're looking at, so it never shows.
V2 · Slightly Dimmed
Still windows sit back 10% in brightness, so the live ones stand out a little.
V3 · A Small Mark on the Bezel
A tiny dot in the bezel's corner: blue when live, silver when still.
