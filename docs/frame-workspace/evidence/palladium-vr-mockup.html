Palladium for the Workspace in VR

Mockups only. Everything is silver-lavender Palladium, lit from the upper left. The laser carries a blue light: near its dot, the metal picks up a blue sheen, and the piece it selects glows. Held pieces press in and glow fully. Depth is real in the headset: the frame, handles and pill sit on their own layers a few centimetres in front of the window, so each eye sees them at a different offset; the shading is drawn in.

1 · Frame Around the Window

The window seen from a step to its left, the way you would see depth in the room.

F1 · Hairline Rim
A slim 1 cm raised rim with a chiseled groove where it meets the window. Lies on the window, so it bends with any curve.
F2 · Machined BezelRECOMMENDED
A 2.5 cm bezel with an inward chamfer, floating 1.5 cm in front with a soft shadow on the window edge. Works curved or flat.
F3 · Deep Frame
F2 with real side walls 4 cm deep, like a physical monitor. Flat windows only: a curved window falls back to F2.

2 · Move Pill and Close

Shown at the window's bottom edge, at rest, highlighted and held. Both are tighter than now: the pill is about a third shorter and sits 3 cm under the frame instead of 6 cm, raised 2 cm in front of it.

P1 · Separate Disc, Raised X
visionOS's layout. The X stands up from the disc as a beveled ridge, with its upper faces lit.
P2 · One Capsule, Engraved XRECOMMENDED
The X sits at the capsule's left end, cut in like Palladium's chiseled divider, with a groove before the grip. Closing and moving are still separate targets.
P3 · One Capsule, Raised X
P2's capsule with P1's raised X, if you prefer the X standing up.
P4 · Separate Disc, Engraved X
P1's layout with the X cut in.

3 · Resize Corners and Curve Grips

Machined pieces that sit on the frame, at rest, highlighted and held. The curve grip is the Palladium resize grip itself, standing upright beside the frame.

H1 · Slide Out on ApproachRECOMMENDED
Hidden behind the frame until the laser leaves the window towards them, then they slide out 1.5 cm in front, as now. The frame stays clean.
H2 · Engraved, Raised on Approach
Faint engraved marks always show where the handles are (left). When the laser comes near, the handles rise out of them (right).

4 · Together

F2, P2 and H1 in the room. The curve grip and right corners have slid out because the laser is past the right edge; the pill is always there.

Highlighted
The laser just past the right edge: the grip under it glows, and the bezel nearby catches its blue light.
Held
Dragging the lower right corner: it presses in and glows fully; the other pieces stay at rest.
