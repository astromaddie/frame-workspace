Palladium in VR: Handles, Round Two

With F2 Machined Bezel and P4 Separate Disc, Engraved X. Corners and side grips now share one design language. They slide out thin and simple, widen with grip texture when the laser is on them, and light up only once clicked to drag: pressed in, they glow from inside, the light bouncing off the walls of the recess rather than spilling out. The laser works as in Stargate: no dot, just a tiny white-hot core where it lands, and the metal around that point brightens in its own colour with a blue cast, fading over a few centimetres. Faces turned towards the tip catch the most, and the bezel nearby catches some too. The pill and X follow the same rule. Shading here is real lighting from the upper left, so it stays correct at every corner.

Corner and Side Grip

Each corner is one continuous L with a rounded bend, its grip texture set at 45° across the bend. The side grip is the same piece, straight.

Slid Out · Laser On It · Clicked to Drag
Slid out: a 6 mm silver-lavender line, no texture. Laser on it: it widens to 15 mm and shows its grip texture, lit only around the laser's tip. Clicked to drag: it presses in (the bevel flips, as in Palladium) and glows from inside until you let go, brightest along the recess's walls; nothing spills out around it.
